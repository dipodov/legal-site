Privacy Policy

Updated September 20, 2026

This policy describes how Dipodov handles information in its iOS video studio (com.dipodov.studio) and on these legal pages.

Your clips, drafts, finished videos, favorites, notes, and Video Credit balance are kept in the app's storage on your device. Dipodov has no account system, social publishing service, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK.

1. Information used by the app

The app stores videos you record or select, generated thumbnails, titles, editing choices, saved results, favorite filters, private showcase selections, and notes. It also stores your credit balance, verified purchase identifiers, and transaction identifiers needed to prevent duplicate delivery. We do not receive your payment card details.

2. Why information is processed

Information is used to import and preview clips, apply color filters, add bundled music, render and export videos, resume drafts, organize your collection, save notes, and deliver purchased credits. These editing operations run on your device. No user clip, image, note, or speech recording is sent by Dipodov to a remote generation service.

3. Camera, photos, microphone, and speech

  • Camera: requested when you record a video. Camera capture records video without microphone audio.
  • Photos: Choose Video opens Apple's system picker, which shares the video you select without giving Dipodov general access to your photo library.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: requested only for optional Dictate Note. Recognition requires on-device support. Speech audio is processed temporarily and is not retained by Dipodov; the note is stored if you save it. You can type instead.
  • Export: the system share sheet sends your finished video to the destination you choose. That destination handles its copy under its own policies.

You can change camera, microphone, and speech permissions in iOS Settings. The app does not request tracking or notification permission.

4. Images of faces

Your selected videos may show faces. Dipodov processes them as ordinary video frames to display previews, apply color adjustments, create thumbnails, and export your chosen result. It does not extract facial geometry, create biometric templates, identify people, match identities, or score emotions. Media depicting faces stays in local app storage unless you choose to export it. Bundled inspiration portraits are static artwork and do not represent accounts or people available to contact.

5. Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes consumable purchases and may handle payment, transaction, and account information under its own privacy policy. Dipodov uses verified StoreKit results to deliver credits and records transaction identifiers locally to avoid granting a purchase twice. Refund or revocation information may remove unused credits associated with that purchase. Apple's records are controlled by Apple and are not erased by clearing Dipodov data.

6. Sharing and website hosting

Dipodov does not sell your content or personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or upload it to a developer-operated content server. You decide whether to share an exported video. The public copies of these policies are hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting this website sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser request, to GitHub as the hosting provider. These pages include no analytics, embedded trackers, or external fonts. The app's bundled legal pages load locally in WKWebView without visiting the hosted copy.

7. Retention, deletion, and your controls

Local content remains until you delete it or clear app data. You can delete finished videos and notes, change favorites, and use Settings → Clear All Local Data. Clearing removes clips, drafts, results, favorites, notes, and remaining credits; transaction identifiers are retained locally solely to prevent duplicate purchase grants. These identifiers are not used as a marketing profile. Deleting the app removes its app storage, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior. Dipodov provides no account recovery or cloud synchronization service. Copies you previously exported and backups managed by you or Apple must be managed separately.

8. Security and choices

Local information uses the iOS app sandbox and device protections. Keep your device protected and export important work before deleting data. No storage system is guaranteed to be secure or permanent. Because we do not maintain a server-side copy of your videos, we cannot retrieve lost local content. Contact us for help with access, deletion, or other privacy questions.

9. Children and policy updates

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service. A parent or guardian can contact us for assistance with local-data removal. We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and revise the date above.

10. Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact qer@gmail.com. Please do not send payment card details or private videos unless they are necessary for your request and you choose to share them.
