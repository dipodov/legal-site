1. What Dipodov provides

You can record or import a clip, choose a color filter and bundled soundtrack, preview the result, and render an MP4. The chosen music replaces the original soundtrack. Drafts, finished videos, favorite filters, private showcase selections, and notes are stored on your device. There is no account registration or public social feed. Adding a video to your showcase does not publish it to other people.
